About Me

My name is Katherine Schlueter and I am a second year undergrad student at the S.I Newhouse School of Communications majoring in Journalism with a focus on Digital Journalism

I currently work for University Girl Magazine as a Digital Editor.

I want people to feel seen, especially when institutions overlook them. As a journalist, I’m drawn to the space where data meets narrative and reporting that brings clarity to rumor, context to cultural moments, and humanity to big systems. .
