How did Texas vote in 2024?

Donald Trump won 242 of Texas's 254 counties in the 2024 presidential election. Kamala Harris won 12, including the state's most populous counties of Harris, Dallas, Bexar and Travis. Hover over any county on the map to see the winner and the vote totals.

Source: Texas Legislative Council, 2024 general election results. Precinct-level results were added up by county for this map.
